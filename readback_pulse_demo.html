Readback Pulse & Linear Superposition

Lorentzian isolated pulse model — PR channel front end
superposed readback signal individual bit pulses ISI-flipped sample
Each written '1' transition contributes an isolated Lorentzian pulse V(x) = V0 / (1 + (2x/PW50)²). The channel output is the linear superposition of every pulse in the sequence. As PW50/T grows, neighboring pulses overlap and distort the amplitude sampled at each bit center — that's inter-symbol interference (ISI). Try pushing PW50/T above ~2 with an alternating 101010 pattern to see samples drop toward zero, or a lone 1 in a sea of zeros to see the isolated pulse recover cleanly.
